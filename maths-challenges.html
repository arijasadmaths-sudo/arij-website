How I teach

Learn to make progress with an unfamiliar problem.

Being ahead in the school syllabus does not always make challenge questions straightforward. Often the key is to draw a useful diagram, test a small case or spot a simple structure before reaching for more advanced mathematics.

I use the student's attempts to guide each lesson, with carefully judged hints, discussion of different approaches and targeted work between sessions. Preparation can focus on a particular competition or on developing problem-solving skills over a longer period.

More about my teaching

  • Accuracy and competition techniqueChoose questions sensibly, use time well and review both errors and unnecessarily long methods.

AIME — American Invitational Mathematics Examination

Open the full AIME paper archive →

Papers and worked solutions from 1983. Papers, solutions and available thresholds side by side.

Advanced problem-solving tuition for students moving on from AMC 10 or AMC 12. Lessons develop algebra, geometry, number theory, counting and probability, with a focus on finding a route through longer problems and carrying an argument through accurately.

15 integer-answer problems · Answers from 0 to 999

Past papers and solutions

AIME problems and solutions — AoPS archive. Choose a year and AIME I or AIME II paper in the community-maintained archive.

Preparing for 2027: the US/Canada competition uses two 90-minute parts, with eight problems followed by seven. Older AIME I and II papers remain useful for practice, but use the current timing for final preparation. International students should check their local organiser’s arrangements. Official AIME format and eligibility.

Qualification and scores

AIME does not have UKMT-style Bronze, Silver and Gold boundaries. The AMC scores needed for entry are listed in the AMC sections above; progression to USAJMO or USAMO follows the relevant year’s selection rules.
