STEP tuition/STEP archive

STEP past papers & solutions.

Choose a year, open the paper and work through the solutions alongside its grade boundaries. Explore STEP 2 and STEP 3, the historical STEP 1 archive and free preparation resources.

STEP 2 and STEP 3 are the current papers. STEP 1 was last sat in 2019 and was cancelled in 2020. Older papers use historical syllabuses and formats.

Boundaries are the minimum marks for grades S, 1, 2 and 3, out of 120. Use the boundaries for the exact year and paper. N/A means unavailable or not applicable.

Extended solutions appear first where available. Community discussions may be incomplete; specific gaps are noted below. MEI provides worked solutions through the guest access described on its website.

Individual STEP tuition packages

One-to-one online lessons, tailored problem sheets and structured practice between lessons, planned around your goals and the areas you need to strengthen.
