ESAT papers & solutions.

Prepare with official ESAT practice, then build your problem-solving skills with ENGAA, NSAA and PAT papers. Find questions, solutions and score guidance together, plus British Physics Olympiad resources.

Current ESAT practice · legacy papers from 200648 paper entriesChecked 10 September 2026

ESAT has five modules: Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2, Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Each has 27 questions in 40 minutes. Most courses require Mathematics 1 and two further modules; check the official course list for your combination.

ESAT reports a separate score from 1.0 to 9.0 for each module. There is no pass/fail grade or universal raw-mark conversion. Use the official score guidance below.

ENGAA, NSAA and PAT are historical tests. Their timing, topics and calculator rules can differ from ESAT. Oxford Physics now uses ESAT; use PAT as additional practice. N/A means unavailable or not applicable.
